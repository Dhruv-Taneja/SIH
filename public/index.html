How it works

1

Submit

Upload JPG / PNG / PDF or capture a live scan with your camera.

2

OCR (PaddleOCR)

OpenCV preprocessing (denoise + threshold + upscale), then PaddleOCR extracts text with per-line confidence.

3

Syntax check

Regex + format rules: Aadhaar 12-digit + Verhoeff checksum, PAN ABCDE1234F, passport, dates, PIN codes…

4

Semantic check

TF-IDF template similarity (scikit-learn), entity completeness, date logic, coherence scoring → verdict.
